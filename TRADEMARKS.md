# Startclip name and logo

Startclip's source code and its logo files are free and open source under the [MIT License](LICENSE). You can use,
change and share them, including in your own fork or product. The S in the logo is cut from Fira Sans ExtraBold, used
under the SIL Open Font License.

The MIT License lets you copy the files. It doesn't let you use the **Startclip name or logo** as the name or brand of
your own version. They are the project's trademarks, kept by browser-b0X for the official project. This covers the
name, the logo files in `assets/`, the favicon, the banners and social preview in `docs/`, and any name or logo close
enough to be mistaken for them.

## Why

People paste Startclip's commands into their own terminals. If a modified copy could call itself Startclip, someone
could change those commands and pass it off as this project. Keeping the name for the official project lets people tell
the real thing from a fork.

## What's fine without asking

- Saying that your project is "based on Startclip" or "a fork of Startclip", in plain text, as long as it's clear it is
  not the official project.
- Writing about Startclip, reviewing it, or linking to this repository, including showing the logo next to that
  coverage.
- Keeping the name and logo in an unmodified copy of this repository, or in a fork that exists only to send changes back
  here.

## What needs a different name

If you publish or share a modified version (a fork, a repackaged download, a hosted copy):

- Give it a different name that isn't confusingly similar to Startclip.
- Replace the logo and favicon with your own.
- Don't suggest that browser-b0X made, reviewed or endorses your version.

## Official sources

The only official source is this repository,
[github.com/browser-b0X/startclip](https://github.com/browser-b0X/startclip). Copies offered anywhere else under the
Startclip name are not from this project.

To ask about using the name or logo in another way, open an issue in this repository.
